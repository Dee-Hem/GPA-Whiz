GPA Whiz is an offline-first academic command centre for students.

Track your GPA and CGPA with a what-if simulator to plan future semesters. Manage your transcript, courses, and grades in one place. Keep your timetable and academic calendar organized with local reminders.

Scholarship & Opportunity Tracker: save and monitor scholarship applications from discovery to outcome. Track deadlines, feedback dates, required documents, and application status through structured stages, from not started to awarded or rejected. Export your applications to PDF or Excel.

GPA Whiz works completely offline. No account, no cloud sync, no internet permission required. Your academic data stays on your device.
